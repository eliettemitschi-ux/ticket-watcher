Ticket Watcher

Watching for sold-out shows to open up.

What is this?

This page watches a handful of currently sold-out shows, gigs and screenings around London and checks each one every ~10 minutes for tickets to actually open up — a returns batch, a day-ticket release, a scheduled re-drop, anything. The moment a watched date/time genuinely becomes bookable, a push notification goes out immediately, so you don't have to keep refreshing a venue's site yourself.

Get notified yourself

Anyone can subscribe to the same alerts this page sends — no account or login needed, just the free ntfy app.

  1. Install ntfy: iPhone (App Store) · Android (Google Play)
  2. Open the app and tap + (Subscribe to topic)
  3. Enter this topic name exactly: loading…

No app, or on a laptop? Subscribe in a browser instead at ntfy.sh/<topic> and leave that tab open.
