What is this?

This page watches a handful of currently sold-out shows, gigs and screenings around London and checks each one every ~10 minutes for tickets to actually open up — a returns batch, a day-ticket release, a scheduled re-drop, anything. The moment a watched date/time genuinely becomes bookable, a push notification goes out immediately, so you don't have to keep refreshing a venue's site yourself.

Worth knowing: venues run their own booking systems in ways that are beyond mine (and Claude's) understanding — some show one overall status for a performance even when it's really split across several price tiers or seating sections. So a show might read as bookable here, and you'll get the notification, but by the time you're on the venue's own site and on the right date, that specific ticket may already be gone again. Always double-check on the venue's page itself before assuming a ticket is definitely there.

How to get notified

Anyone can subscribe to the same alerts this page sends — no account or login needed, just the free ntfy app (or a browser tab). Two ways to subscribe:

Option A — everything on this page

  1. Install ntfy: iPhone (App Store) · Android (Google Play)
  2. Open the app and tap + (Subscribe to topic)
  3. Enter this topic name exactly: loading…

No app, or on a laptop? Subscribe in a browser instead at ntfy.sh/<topic> and leave that tab open.

Option B — just one specific show

Only care about one show? Each one below has its own 🔔 Notify me for just this show link — subscribe to that instead of the topic above and you'll only hear about that one.
