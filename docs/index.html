What is this?

This page watches a handful of currently sold-out shows, gigs and screenings around London and checks each one every ~5 minutes for tickets to actually open up — a returns batch, a day-ticket release, a scheduled re-drop, anything. The moment a watched date/time genuinely becomes bookable, a push notification goes out immediately, so you don't have to keep refreshing a venue's site yourself.

Worth knowing: venues run their own booking systems in ways that are beyond mine (and Claude's) understanding — some show one overall status for a performance even when it's really split across several price tiers or seating sections. So a show might read as bookable here, and you'll get the notification, but by the time you're on the venue's own site and on the right date, that specific ticket may already be gone again. Always double-check on the venue's page itself before assuming a ticket is definitely there.

How to get notified

There are two ways to subscribe — pick whichever fits what you want to hear about. Both use the free ntfy app (or just a browser tab), no account or login needed either way.

Option A — everything on this page

  1. Install ntfy: iPhone (App Store) · Android (Google Play)
  2. Open the app and tap + (Subscribe to topic)
  3. Enter this topic name exactly: loading…

No app, or on a laptop? Subscribe in a browser instead at ntfy.sh/<topic> and leave that tab open — it'll show a popup the moment anything on this page changes.

Option B — just one specific show

Only care about one of the shows below? Scroll to its card and tap its own 🔔 Notify me for just this show link instead of following the steps above — that subscribes you to a topic for that show alone, so you won't hear about any of the others.

Settings

Install as an app

Add this page to your home screen and it opens full-screen like a normal app, with its own icon. No app store and no account. Notifications still come from the free ntfy app, installing doesn't change that.

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari (links opened inside WhatsApp, Instagram or Mail won't offer it: tap the ⋯ or compass icon and choose Open in Safari first).
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up), or the ⋯ menu, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on if you see it, then tap Add.
Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome (if it opened inside WhatsApp or another app, tap ⋮ and choose Open in Chrome).
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones it's called Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install.

Samsung Internet: tap the ☰ menu, then Add page to, then Home screen.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Look for the small install icon at the right end of the address bar and click it.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose Install / Apps → Install this site as an app.
